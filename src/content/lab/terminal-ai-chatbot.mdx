## What I built

A terminal-based chatbot with conversation history, built using Groq's free-tier API via its OpenAI-compatible SDK.

## What I learned

Message array mechanics and role-based prompting, plus exploring the fundamentals of transformer architecture. This was Week 1 of a self-designed 12-week AI engineering roadmap built on my existing JavaScript/Node.js stack.
